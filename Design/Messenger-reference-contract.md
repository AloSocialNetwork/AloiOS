# Messenger reference contract

Scope: iOS conversation, composer, attachments, poll composer and feed action geometry. Keep the existing inbox without category filters, the initial-avatar palette and Alo's black/blue style. Do not redesign the profile as part of the reaction pass.

Reference: the user's Telegram iOS screenshots in this task. Native points, not screenshot pixels, determine layout. Do not globally zoom screens to match an image.

Primary visual target: large iPhone Pro Max. Validate the 375 pt compact layout afterward for clipping and operability, not as the size to impose on all phones.

- Chat header: independent 44 pt back button, centred 48 pt name/status capsule, 44 pt avatar on the right. Name uses the system headline size (17 pt at default), status caption (12 pt). Native material, a translucent dark tint and a soft shadow separate the controls from the wallpaper; Reduce Transparency has an opaque fallback.
- Composer: 44 pt tap areas; 17 pt native text; stable trailing tool space before/after first character. No zero-distance drag or scale gesture attached to editable text. Edge back navigation must not intercept selection inside the input.
- Attachments: one panel with a single top grabber, centred title and 44 pt close/done controls below the status bar. The inline native Photos picker lives in an independent UIHostingController, so its sheet preferences cannot move the outer header or introduce an inner detent. The grid follows the header in both collapsed and expanded states. Selected category background covers icon and label. No fabricated recent files/photos.
- Message widths use the actual viewport, including the 36 pt selection gutter, rather than the device's nominal screen size. Selection owns the row's hit testing and accessibility, including media buttons.
- Reactions: content-sized, left-anchored wrapping chips without outlines; selected chips use Alo blue. A 32 pt visible pill has at least a 44 pt tap area. There is no additional vertical row gap. Text/forward/media content determines bubble width independently of reactions; adding, replacing or removing a reaction must not rewrap that content or move its timestamp into a different layout. A footer adds only its natural rows, with 2 pt separation. Voice/poll/circle retain their fixed content widths. The conversation and context preview share the footer. Circle reactions occupy a real row below the video, not an offset overlay. Show all server-provided reaction groups rather than silently taking three. Compact large counts visually, retaining full counts in accessibility.
- Feed: four stable action columns, 44 pt touch targets, monospaced compact counters. Like/repost feedback animates the icon, never scales the card or shifts neighbouring controls. Preserve the post body's geometry when counts cross digit boundaries.
- Context menu: dim and blur the conversation; keep the focused preview sharp, retaining reply/forward attribution. Apply shadows after clipping so they are visible. Menu icons are unboxed; actions have 44 pt targets. Measure the preview's actual height; on short screens scroll the actions instead of chopping the preview's last reaction row. Long-press must not also trigger playback or open media, including when the finger is held past the animation cooldown.
- Voice: resample real waveform levels across the available width, retaining the final samples. Missing waveform data is flat, not invented; unknown duration is a dash until measured from the asset.
- Messenger profile: show the large name once beside the avatar area; reveal the compact name/status in the safe-area header after the large title scrolls away. Back remains reachable. Pin the media tabs below this header while browsing a long grid. Opening and closing media must retain the profile's tab and scroll position. A cancelled profile swipe stays in the profile; a completed swipe returns only one level, to the chat.
- Media viewer: show the current position for multi-item galleries. Pause video when its page becomes inactive, not just when SwiftUI eventually removes an off-screen page. Keep the close control below system chrome.
- Poll: 17 pt fields and toggles, 13 pt secondary section labels, grouped 20 pt corners, inset16/gap24, minimum44 pt actions; preserve quiz, anonymity, multiple answers, scheduling and auto-close.
- Motion: 120 ms opacity press feedback; short interruptible selection movement; honour Reduce Motion. Keep native keyboard timing.
- Data: all normal app flows remain server-backed. `ALO_UI_MESSENGER_PREVIEW` is an explicit DEBUG-only UI-test fixture, not account data and not stored in the account cache. Screenshots from it must be labelled as test data.

Verification: launch real components in the simulator, capture chat/keyboard/attachment/poll states, check cursor entry and stable input width, and traverse attachment-to-poll presentation. Record limitations rather than claiming unsupported actions were tested.
